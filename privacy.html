Albiondle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09/09/2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Albiondle game (“Albiondle,” “the Game,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects when you use it, how that information is used, and who it is shared with. Albiondle is a small, free, single-player puzzle game about the maps and abilities of Albion Online, playable in a web browser and as a Discord Activity.

Who runs this game. Albiondle is operated by Arepha (“the operator”). It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Sandbox Interactive GmbH, Albion Online, or Discord Inc. Questions about this policy can be sent to raphael.robin31000@gmail.com.

1. Summary

Albiondle is deliberately lightweight. It has no accounts and no login, it does not ask you to create a profile, and it does not track you across other websites or serve advertising. The only information you actively send us is what you choose to type into an optional “Report wrong data” form. Everything else is limited to the ordinary technical information any website receives, plus small values stored on your own device.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you choose to submit (bug reports)

The Game includes an optional “Report wrong data” feature. If — and only if — you fill it in and submit it, we receive the text you enter together with basic context about the puzzle you were on. This may include:

  • the name of the map or ability you are reporting;
  • which part of the game you were in;
  • the field you think is wrong, what you believe it should be, and any free-text notes you add;
  • the date and time of the submission.

This form is free text. Please do not enter personal information about yourself or others into it — it isn’t needed and we don’t want it. Report submissions are sent to and stored in a Google Sheet via a Google Apps Script endpoint (see “How information is shared,” below).

2.2 Information collected automatically (technical data)

Like any website or web application, when the Game loads it and the services that host or supply its files receive standard technical information, which can include your device’s IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, and the date and time of requests. This happens at the level of our hosting provider and the third-party services the Game loads resources from; we do not use this information to identify you, and we do not build a profile from it.

Discord Activities note. When you run Albiondle as a Discord Activity, it loads inside Discord like an embedded website. As Discord itself notes, this means the Game (and the services it relies on) can see technical information such as your IP address. Running the Game does not give it access to your Discord account information unless you are explicitly asked to authorize that and agree.

2.3 Information stored on your device (local storage)

The Game may store small values in your browser’s local storage — for example, a draft of a report you haven’t submitted, or which items you’ve reviewed. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us as part of normal play, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

2.4 Discord information

The current version of the Game does not request access to your Discord account and does not collect your Discord username, user ID, avatar, email, servers, or friends. If a future version offers an optional feature that uses your Discord identity (for example, to show your name in a shared game), Discord will show you an authorization screen describing exactly what is requested, and that feature will only receive what you approve. This policy will be updated before any such feature is enabled.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • No account, password, email, or contact details (unless you email us directly).
  • No payment or financial information — the Game is free.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No precise location data.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Game — technical data is used to deliver the Game’s files and make it work in your browser or in Discord.
  • To fix data errors — report submissions are used to correct wrong map/ability information in the Game.
  • To keep the Game secure and reliable — technical logs may be used to diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and maintain availability.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Cookies

The Game does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It may use browser local storage as described in section 2.3. Third-party services listed above may set their own cookies or receive technical data according to their policies.

6. Data retention

Report submissions are kept for as long as they are useful for correcting the Game’s data, after which they may be deleted. Technical logs held by our hosting and third-party providers are retained according to those providers’ own policies. You can ask us to delete a report you submitted (see section 8).

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, and rely on the security of established providers (Discord, Google, and our hosting provider) for data they process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA).

  • Reports: because reports are free text, we usually cannot link a submission to you. If you tell us the approximate time and content of a report you submitted, we will make reasonable efforts to locate and delete it on request.
  • Local storage: you can clear it yourself at any time via your browser settings.
  • Requests: contact raphael.robin31000@gmail.com to make a rights request. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

9. Children’s privacy

Albiondle is not directed to children under 13, and Discord itself requires users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact raphael.robin31000@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. International users

The Game and the services it uses may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union. By using the Game you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations, which may have different data protection rules than your home country.

11. Third-party links and services

The Game and this policy may reference third-party services (such as Discord, Google, and Albion Online). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties; their own policies govern your use of their services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above, and material changes will be reflected before they take effect. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
